Title: Communication Guidelines
Role: all
Date: 2026-09-24
----------------------------------------

1. Channel Standards
• Slack: Primary platform for internal real-time and asynchronous messaging. Use public channels by default; reserve DMs for 1-on-1s and private feedback.
• Email: Reserved for external vendors, formal HR announcements, and legal communication.
• Zoom / Google Meet: Used for synchronous meetings with video-on preference during standups.
• Notion / Confluence: Single source of truth for documentation, RFCs, and engineering specifications.

2. Slack Etiquette
Always use message threads to keep channels readable. Use status indicators (e.g. 🌴 Vacation, 🎧 Deep Work, 🥪 Lunch) to manage availability expectations.
